# Launch Pack: plan and tracker

Working name: **Ship It Twice** (placeholder, rename freely).
One-line pitch: *How I got an AI + subscription + health app through App Store and Google Play review as a solo dev, with the checklists and templates I actually used.*

## Why this product
- Fastest route to a first sale found in the research: no app review, no inventory, no support burden.
- Only credible if it comes from real experience. It does: Core+ (Expo/React Native, RevenueCat, HealthKit and Health Connect, Gemini AI, Supabase, 5 languages, 80+ versions via EAS).
- Niche is specific: **Expo / React Native solo devs shipping subscription, AI or health apps.** Reachable in r/reactnative, r/expo, r/iOSProgramming, r/androiddev, r/indiehackers, X, dev.to.
- Core+ doubles as the case study and a cross-sell.

## Honest expectations (from the research)
- First sale in 1-2 weeks only if you post consistently where these devs gather.
- About $200-800 in month one, $500-1,500/mo by months 3-6. Small niche, so treat as a cash and credibility engine.
- Rules change. Every policy claim must be re-checked against the official page before it ships, and the pack needs a "last verified" date and periodic updates.

## Time budget
2-3 hrs/day is about 15 hrs/week. All tasks below are async and can be done in chunks.

| Phase | When | Tasks | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Write v1 | Week 1 | Fill `pack-outline.md` from your own review history. Verify each policy item. | ~15 |
| 2. Storefront | Week 2 | Landing page, checkout (Lemon Squeezy or Stripe link), free checklist lead magnet, queue 2 weeks of posts from `content-bank.md`. | ~15 |
| 3. Soft launch | Weeks 2-3 | Post at launch price, collect feedback and testimonials. | ~10 |
| 4. Engine | Weeks 3-8 | One weekly batch session (~6 hrs): make and schedule 5-7 posts, answer questions, update pack. | ~6/wk |
| 5. Second product | Month 2+ | Only if sales appear. | open |

## Tracker
- [ ] Decide final name and price ($19 launch, then $29-49)
- [ ] Choose checkout: Lemon Squeezy (handles VAT, ~5% + $0.50) vs Stripe link (you handle sales tax)
- [ ] Fill every `[YOURS]` section in `pack-outline.md` from real experience
- [ ] Verify each item in the "Verify before shipping" list against official docs
- [ ] Build free lead magnet (rejection-risk checklist, 1 page)
- [ ] Landing page live
- [ ] Refund policy and "last verified" date on the product
- [ ] Queue first 2 weeks of posts
- [ ] Launch posts
- [ ] Collect first 3 testimonials (real ones only)

## What not to do
- Do not publish AI-drafted policy claims unverified. Wrong compliance advice is worse than none.
- Do not invent testimonials, sales numbers or revenue claims.
- Do not claim guaranteed approval. Say "what worked for me".
