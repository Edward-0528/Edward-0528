# Content bank

Batch these once a week and schedule them. Every post should come from something you actually did. Each ends with a link to the free checklist or the pack. Pick 5-7 per batch.

## Formats that need no face
- Screen recording with voice or captions
- Screenshot of a real rejection message, then the fix
- Text thread / carousel

## Where to post
- r/reactnative, r/expo, r/iOSProgramming, r/androiddev, r/indiehackers (read each community's self-promotion rules first; lead with value, not links)
- X, dev.to, LinkedIn, short-video platforms

## Cadence
- One batch session per week, 5-7 posts scheduled
- 15-20 minutes a day answering comments and questions (optional, can be skipped on busy retail days)

## Metrics to watch
- Checklist email signups, landing page visits, sales, which hooks drive clicks
- Cut formats that get nothing after 3-4 tries, repeat what works
