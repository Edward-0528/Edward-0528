## What I Built

**Core+** — AI Nutrition and Fitness Tracker (iOS and Android)

Core+ is a production mobile app I designed and shipped solo. Users can photograph 
their food and get instant AI-generated macro breakdowns, track calories against 
live HealthKit and Health Connect data, compete with friends on leaderboards, and 
manage subscriptions through a built-in paywall.

- Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for real-time camera-based food recognition
- Apple HealthKit and Google Health Connect integration
- Supabase backend with PostgreSQL, authentication, and row-level security
- Google OAuth, Apple Sign-In, and biometric login (Face ID, Touch ID, Fingerprint)
- RevenueCat subscription management with paywall and free trial flows
- Social features including friends, leaderboards, squad challenges, and referrals
- Push notifications using Notifee with native scheduling on both platforms
- Localized into 5 languages: English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese
- Shipped 80+ versions using EAS Build and EAS Submit CI/CD pipeline
